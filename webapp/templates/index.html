The Problem

One metric. Two meanings. Silent chaos.

Rosetta is a linter for meaning across your DataHub graph. It finds where two teams silently mean different things by the same metric, quantifies the blast radius, brokers a canonical definition, and writes it back.

🤖
AI Models Fail Silently
An ML model trained on Finance's active_user predicts on Marketing's data. No error. Wrong answers.
📊
Executive Reports Disagree
The CFO sees 2.1M users. The CMO sees 3.8M. Both are right by their definition — and leadership loses trust in data.
🔍
No One Knows It's Happening
DataHub holds the glossary. But when Finance and Marketing define the same term differently, nothing warns you.
